features:
  - title: Designs that reflow
    details: One template fits every label size. Rows, columns, per-size variants and rules decide what moves, shrinks or hides.
  - title: What you see is what prints
    details: The preview is the exact dot image sent to the printer. Checks warn about clipped text, thin barcodes and the printer's edge.
  - title: Prints from your browser
    details: Bluetooth, USB or network printers, straight from Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android phone.
  - title: Labels from your data
    details: Spreadsheets, Google Sheets, JSON APIs and a REST API. Rules pick icons and layouts by row.
  - title: Barcodes done properly
    details: More than 30 kinds, drawn at whole printer dots, with quiet zones checked before you print.
  - title: Yours to run or embed
    details: One container with its own database, or a React component and request handler inside your own app.
